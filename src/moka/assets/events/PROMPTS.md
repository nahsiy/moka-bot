The mascot is joyfully welcoming a group of friends for a coffee round. Add a small tasteful teal barista apron without any text or emblem. Premium rounded 3D illustration, gorgeous soft studio light, tactile cream ceramic, amber coffee and muted teal accents. Strong expressive silhouette and clean detail readable in a Discord card. SQUARE composition with generous 10 percent transparent padding around the ENTIRE mascot, tray, cups and steam. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background, isolated character cutout, no cafe interior, no counter, no floor, no scenery, no backdrop, no vignette, no rectangle, no decorative text, no words, no numbers, no watermarks.
